I'm **Angelito P. Decatoria III**, but most people call me **Thirdy**. I'm a full-stack software engineer and Computer Science graduate from Cavite State University, and I build web, mobile and AI products people can rely on.

Lately I've been building open-source developer tools: **[Vedit](https://github.com/onetwothird/Vedit)**, an AI video editor that cuts the pauses and bad takes out of talking-head footage, and **[CommitGen](https://github.com/onetwothird/CommitGen)**, a CLI that writes your commit messages from the staged diff.

When I'm not coding, you'll probably find me playing Valorant or exploring new technologies.
